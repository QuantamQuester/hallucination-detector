Known limits

  • The benchmark's hallucinated answers are full sentences and its correct answers are short phrases. The model leans on that length difference, so a long correct answer can be flagged.
  • Grounding is lexical. An answer that reuses evidence words in a false statement can pass.
  • It gives a probability to prioritise checking. It cannot establish that a statement is true.
